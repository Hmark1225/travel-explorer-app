# Draft legal, privacy and licensing checklist — human review required

This checklist is preparation for review, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. No affiliate relationship is asserted or activated by Phase 1. Public URLs still involve ordinary external data transfer after confirmation.

- [ ] For each enabled provider and exact URL, verify current linking terms, domain, path, redirects, territory, product relevance, language support and necessary authorization. Record reviewer, date and evidence; define periodic review and revocation ownership.
- [ ] Confirm whether an affiliate agreement is active, whether the public tracking identifier and query format are valid, and whether disclosure wording/placement meets agreement and local advertising requirements. Keep it disabled until approved. Review consumer-protection obligations and avoid claims of best price, discounts or availability.
- [ ] Review provider trademarks and naming. Phase 1 uses plain provider names; it imports no logos, reviews, descriptions or partner photos. Never scrape. Obtain separate content/display/cache rights before API content or branding is introduced.
- [ ] Confirm API access, licence, cache/retention and required attribution for every future field, including images and traveller reviews. Review source-link/branding placement and any restrictions involving the existing map. Expired or revoked content must be removed.
- [ ] Update the app privacy notice for third-party handoffs: the destination/query and standard browser/network data can reach the provider. The provider applies its own privacy terms. Explain language fallback and any provider-side location request; Travel Explorer does not transmit its Saved/Trips lists or request location for these links.
- [ ] Review whether commercial tracking requires consent, opt-out, platform disclosures or store privacy-label changes. Do not treat clicking an ordinary external link as blanket analytics consent.
- [ ] Analytics remains disabled and has no transport. Before enabling it, approve purpose, legal basis, minimization, retention/deletion, aggregate thresholds, access controls, processor terms, international transfers, platform disclosures and incident response. Include IP/server access logs in the review.
- [ ] Verify no server API credentials or service-role secrets enter mobile bundles, repository, logs, examples or analytics. Rotate any accidentally exposed key. Keep only public approved affiliate identifiers in client configuration.
- [ ] Manually check English/Hungarian copy, screen reader reading/focus order, large text, modal cancellation/back behavior, offline failure and browser handoff on physical iOS/Android devices with Expo Go. Check Save/Unsave and Add/Remove Trip before and after a link handoff and a language switch.
- [ ] Agree on support ownership when providers are down or links expire. Check that provider disablement hides actions and the guide, photos, sources, Saved and Trips remain usable.
