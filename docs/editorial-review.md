# Editorial review checklist

All 18 destination summaries and all 156 travel entries need source review. Authored counts are in content-audit.md; photo-by-photo metadata and outstanding fields are in content-inventory.json. No checkmark below represents completed verification.

## Review each destination and entry

- [ ] Read exact official tourism, monument, museum or venue pages; record which factual claims each page supports. Candidate homepages are leads only. Record final URLs, access date, relevant text and reviewer, including redirects and conflicts.
- [ ] Review identification, descriptions and tips. Check that an experience category is clearly labelled and is not a promised tour, vendor endorsement or guaranteed service.
- [ ] Confirm admission classification or retain unknown. Do not add numerical prices, hours, booking claims or availability without a separately maintained process. Durations are editorial planning estimates.
- [ ] Review exact visitor entrance or an appropriate defined starting point where useful. Record source, date and precision; keep null when missing. Do not replace an unknown entrance with an overview point without an explicit label.
- [ ] Review the official website before enabling its button. Check destination, HTTPS, redirects and identity.
- [ ] Confirm authentic photo subject using the original asset and context; reject generated, misleading or unrelated images. Confirm the author/rightsholder and whether the license applies to this exact file.
- [ ] Record original/display/source URLs, author, required credit, license and its URL, commercial-use decision, reviewer, verification dates, evidence and modification notes. Consider attribution, share-alike, modification limits, public-domain jurisdiction and non-copyright rights where relevant. Keep a neutral placeholder if uncertain.
- [ ] Validate credits and source/license links within the app. Metadata checks do not authenticate evidence or guarantee legal compliance.
- [ ] Set lastVerifiedAt/reviewStatus only after actual travel-source review; a gazetteer extraction date or asset review date is not a travel review.
- [ ] Run typecheck, tests, the content inventory check and native exports; perform the physical-device checklist.
